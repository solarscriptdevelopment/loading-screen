Solar Loading Screen
**First Impressions, Fully Loaded.**

Give players something worth watching while they connect. A clean, modern loading screen for FiveM that shows off your server before they even spawn in.

## :sparkles: Features
- :busts_in_silhouette: **Meet the Team**: staff cards with avatars, roles and custom banners.
- :frame_photo: **Gallery**: show off your best screenshots.
- :scroll: **Rules & Updates**: sliding panels to keep players informed.
- :musical_note: **Music Player**: shuffle, skip, seek, volume and album art.
- :link: **Social Cards**: Discord, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and Tebex pop-outs.
- :clapper: **Video Background**: your own MP4 or a YouTube link.
- :bar_chart: **Live Progress Bar**: real loading progress from the game.
- :art: **Custom Colors**: set your own accent colors to match your brand.
- :mouse_three_button: **Custom Cursor**: able to set your own custom cursor shown on all players screens!

## :gear: Easy Setup
Everything is set in **one config file**. Add your logo, team, songs, links and colors. No coding needed.
